UNREAL ENGINE 5 - LANDSCAPE MODE TOOLS (SIMPLE GUIDE)
=====================================================

How to open it:  press Shift+2  (or pick "Landscape" in the mode dropdown, top-left).
Note: names and places can differ a little between Unreal versions.


THE BASICS (REMEMBER THESE)
---------------------------
Left click + drag ........ do the tool's effect (usually RAISE)
Shift + Left click ....... do the OPPOSITE (usually LOWER)
Ctrl + Z ................. undo last stroke
Ctrl + Y ................. redo
Alt + J .................. top-down view (nice for circles)
Alt + G .................. back to normal perspective view

Landscape Mode has 3 tabs:
  MANAGE = make the landscape, change its size
  SCULPT = change the SHAPE (hills, holes, cliffs)
  PAINT  = change the LOOK (rock, sand, grass textures)


=====================================================
BRUSH SETTINGS (SAME FOR MOST TOOLS)
=====================================================
Tool Strength ... how strong each stroke is. Low (0.1-0.3) = gentle. High = fast and rough.
Brush Size ...... how wide the brush is. It is in centimetres: 1000 = 10 metres.
Brush Falloff ... how soft the edge is. High = soft and smooth. Low = hard edge.
Brush Type ...... shape of the brush:
                  Circle    = normal round brush
                  Alpha     = uses a picture (good for craters)
                  Pattern   = repeats a picture
                  Component = affects one whole square piece
Falloff Type .... Smooth, Linear, Spherical, Tip = different edge curves.
                  Smooth is the best one to start with.


=====================================================
TAB 1: MANAGE (SET UP THE LANDSCAPE)
=====================================================

NEW
  Creates a new landscape. Set the size, then click Create.
  Size guide (with scale 100): 8x8 components = 0.5 km, 16x16 = 1 km, 32x32 = 2 km.
  You can also "Import from File" to start from a heightmap picture.

SELECT
  Pick landscape pieces (called components). Other tools use your selection.

ADD
  Adds new pieces next to your landscape. Use it to make the map bigger.

DELETE
  Removes pieces you selected. Use it to cut away parts you do not need.

MOVE TO LEVEL
  Moves selected pieces into another level. Only needed for big streaming worlds.

CHANGE COMPONENT SIZE (RESIZE)
  Changes the size or detail of the landscape pieces.

EDIT SPLINES
  Draws roads, paths or rivers that follow the ground.
  Ctrl + Left click puts down points. The line joins them.


=====================================================
TAB 2: SCULPT (CHANGE THE SHAPE)
=====================================================

SCULPT (the main tool)
  Raises the ground. Shift + click lowers it.
  Use for: mountains, valleys, the crater bowl, trenches.
  Tip: many light passes look better than one heavy stroke.

ERASE (newer versions)
  Removes your sculpt changes and puts the ground back to how it was.
  Use for: undoing a mistake in one area.

SMOOTH
  Softens rough or sharp areas.
  Use for: sharp peaks, jagged slopes, joining two shapes.
  Tip: keep strength LOW (0.05-0.3) so it does not flatten everything.

FLATTEN
  Your first click picks a height. Dragging makes the ground match that height.
  Use for: flat deserts, building spots, landing areas.

RAMP
  Click two points. Unreal draws a smooth slope between them.
  Then click "Add Ramp".
  Use for: paths up a cliff, terraces, roads on hills.
  Settings: Width (how wide), Side Falloff (how soft the sides are).

EROSION
  Wears down steep areas and makes natural cracks and gullies.
  Use for: mountain walls that look weathered, not hand-made.

HYDRO EROSION
  Pretends rain washes the ground and carves channels.
  Use for: river beds, water-cut ravines.

NOISE
  Adds random small bumps and dips.
  Use for: rough, rocky ground.
  Settings: Noise Scale (small or big bumps), Noise Mode (Add, Sub or Both).

RETOPOLOGIZE
  Fixes stretched triangles on very steep slopes.
  Use for: cliffs whose texture looks smeared.

VISIBILITY
  Hides part of the landscape to make a hole.
  Use for: cave entrances. Needs a material set up for it.

MASK / SELECTION
  Paints a mask. Masked areas are protected from your other tools.
  Use for: sculpting next to an area you want to keep safe.

COPY / PASTE
  Copies a piece of terrain and pastes it somewhere else.
  Use for: repeating the same hill or crater shape.

MIRROR
  Copies one half of the landscape onto the other side.
  Use for: symmetrical maps.


=====================================================
TAB 3: PAINT (CHANGE THE LOOK)
=====================================================
You need a landscape MATERIAL with layers first (for example rock, sand, gravel).
Each layer needs a "Layer Info" asset. Then you paint with them.

PAINT
  Paints the chosen layer onto the ground.
  Left click adds the layer. Shift + click removes it.
  Use for: sand in the flats, rock on the walls.

SMOOTH (paint)
  Blends the edges between layers so they do not look sharp.

FLATTEN (paint)
  Evens out the layer strength in an area.

NOISE (paint)
  Mixes layers in a random, natural way.
  Use for: rock slowly turning into sand.


=====================================================
WHICH TOOL FOR WHICH JOB? (QUICK TABLE)
=====================================================
Dig a crater bowl ............ Sculpt + Shift
Build mountains .............. Sculpt (no Shift)
Make steep, natural cliffs ... Smooth, then Erosion
Flat desert floor ............ Flatten
Deep trenches ................ Sculpt + Shift, small brush
Rough rocky hills ............ Noise
Path up a mountain ........... Ramp
Water-cut gullies ............ Hydro Erosion
Fix smeared cliff texture .... Retopologize
Roads and paths .............. Edit Splines
Make the map bigger .......... Add
Cave hole .................... Visibility
Paint sand and rock .......... Paint tab > Paint


=====================================================
GOOD HABITS
=====================================================
1. Set up the sun first. Shadows help you see the shapes.
2. Use big brushes first (large shapes), small brushes last (details).
3. Use low strength and many passes.
4. Save often (Ctrl+S).
5. Smooth lightly at the end to remove sharp spikes.
